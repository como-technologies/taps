One reviewed, versioned record of one decision: its context, the options
considered, the outcome, and the consequences — with an explicit lifecycle
status carried in the page frontmatter and transitioned only by adroit.
The scope is deliberately broader than architecture: if it changes how the
team builds or operates, it belongs in the corpus. How the team then
*practices* what was decided belongs in a [[glossary/guide]].
