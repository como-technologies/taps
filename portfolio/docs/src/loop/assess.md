# Assess

The loop opens with evidence. Structured interviews with your engineers,
architects, and platform owners become a consistent maturity model:
domains, practices, and the binary questions that decide where you
actually stand — not where the org chart says you stand.

## assessments

An AI-assisted authoring environment for maturity assessments. Your
subject-matter experts co-create the assessment with an assistant through
a guided workflow — the assistant drafts, the experts correct and steer —
and the whole pipeline can run on a local model, so client material never
has to leave client infrastructure.

**What you get.** A schema-validated assessment document (YAML, JSON, or
TOML). Not a deck: the export is exactly what the
[Prescribe](./prescribe.md) stage consumes to seed proposed decisions, so
the hand-off is a file format, not a re-keying exercise.

Details, walkthroughs, and the full authoring pipeline live in the
[assessments repo](https://github.com/como-technologies/assessments).
