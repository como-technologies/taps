# Services

The tools carry the mechanics; the services carry the people. What turns
the portfolio into an engagement is Como's services layer — facilitation,
authoring, enablement, and the occasional hard conversation with a
stakeholder who'd rather keep the status quo. Every engagement runs the
full loop by default, and each service stands alone for clients who
already have their own shape: bring your own assessment framework, your
own knowledge base, or your own measurement, and Como adapts around it.
What you can't skip is the thread itself.

## Assessment engagement

Facilitated discovery that ends in a machine-consumable artifact, not a
deck. Como runs structured interviews with the people who own your
systems; AI-assisted synthesis turns the conversation into a consistent
maturity model your experts review and sign off. On a repeat engagement,
the previous cycle's measurements are the starting evidence — which is
what makes the loop a loop.

## Knowledge base authoring

Co-creation of a knowledge base tailored to your context. Como configures
your harness of choice from the authoring kit, seeds proposed decisions
from the assessment, and facilitates the sessions where your architects
accept, reject, and refine them. What the engagement hands over is not a
document set but a working loop: the knowledge base, the configured
harness, and people who have run it.

## Adoption enablement

Pairing, workshops, and incremental rollout that get accepted decisions
into daily practice — the stage where programs usually stall, and where
Como spends the most effort. Deliberately human-led: what to roll out
first, what to defer, and when a decision needs to go back to Prescribe
because reality disagreed with it.

## Agentic delivery

Pick a set of accepted decisions; each comes back as a reviewable,
decision-tagged pull request in your own forge, worked by the
[conduit](./loop/adopt.md) engine with your reviewers holding the scope,
review, and merge gates. You never have to trust an agent — you have to
review a pull request, which your team already knows how to do.

## Measurement setup

Standing up the Measure stage so adoption is observed, not assumed:
baseline capture, the monthly cadence, the effort-labeling habit, and the
capacity and sentiment instruments. The deliverable is evidence that
feeds the next assessment — the loop's last hand-off, delivered as plain
machine-readable files.
