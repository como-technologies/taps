# Assessment: Treatment Decision Quality

> Assess a healthcare system's quality of treatment decision-making in specialty therapeutic areas to ensure optimal patient outcomes.

**Goal:** Identify opportunities to improve evidence-based treatment selection, biomarker utilization, and health equity across the patient population.

---

## Domain: Biomarker-Driven Selection

**Context:** How molecular and genetic testing informs treatment decisions in precision medicine
**Value:** Biomarker-driven care improves outcomes, reduces futile treatment, and enables precision medicine
**Risk:** Undertesting leads to missed targeted therapy opportunities; patients receive suboptimal treatment

### Practice: Testing Completeness

**Context:** Ensuring all relevant patients receive appropriate biomarker testing
**Value:** Complete testing identifies all patients who could benefit from targeted therapies
**Risk:** Gaps in testing leave patients with actionable markers untreated or inappropriately treated

#### Questions

- [ ] Are all eligible patients tested for guideline-recommended biomarkers?
  - *Track testing rates for BRCA, HER2, PD-L1, MSI-H, etc. by indication*

- [ ] Is there a process to identify patients who should be tested but haven't been?
  - *Retrospective chart review, EHR alerts for untested eligible patients*

- [ ] Are testing rates monitored by provider, site, and patient demographic?
  - *Identify variation that may indicate access or awareness gaps*

- [ ] Is there reflex testing for standard markers at time of diagnosis?
  - *Automatic testing without requiring separate orders*

- [ ] Are testing rates compared to benchmarks or quality standards?
  - *ASCO QOPI, payer quality measures, internal benchmarks*

### Practice: Result Integration

**Context:** How biomarker results are incorporated into treatment planning
**Value:** Testing only adds value when results appropriately inform treatment decisions
**Risk:** Results that don't reach decision-makers or aren't acted upon waste resources and harm patients

#### Questions

- [ ] Are biomarker results available in time to inform treatment decisions?
  - *Results before first treatment; monitor turnaround time*

- [ ] Are results documented in a structured, searchable format?
  - *Not just scanned reports; discrete data fields for key markers*

- [ ] Is there a process for discussing complex or uncertain results?
  - *Molecular tumor board, expert consultation for variants of uncertain significance*

- [ ] Are results communicated to patients with appropriate counseling?
  - *Especially for hereditary findings with family implications*

- [ ] Is there documentation of how results influenced treatment selection?
  - *Audit trail connecting result to decision*

### Practice: Mutation-Specific Pathways

**Context:** Treatment protocols tailored to specific molecular findings
**Value:** Precise matching of treatment to biology optimizes outcomes
**Risk:** Generic treatment despite actionable findings fails precision medicine promise

#### Questions

- [ ] Are there defined treatment pathways for each actionable mutation?
  - *BRCA → PARP inhibitor; EGFR → TKI; etc.*

- [ ] Are combination biomarker profiles addressed in pathways?
  - *Co-mutations, resistance patterns, complex profiles*

- [ ] Is there guidance for mutations without approved targeted therapy?
  - *Clinical trial referral, off-label considerations, expanded access*

- [ ] Are resistance mutations assessed and addressed at progression?
  - *T790M, ESR1, AR-V7 testing at appropriate timepoints*

- [ ] Is there tracking of outcomes by biomarker subgroup?
  - *Monitor whether biomarker-driven care achieves expected benefits*

## Domain: Evidence-Based Protocols

**Context:** Alignment of treatment decisions with clinical evidence and guidelines
**Value:** Evidence-based care optimizes outcomes and ensures patients receive best available treatments
**Risk:** Idiosyncratic or outdated treatment patterns lead to suboptimal outcomes

### Practice: Guideline Adherence

**Context:** Consistency of care with NCCN, ESMO, and other authoritative guidelines
**Value:** Guidelines synthesize evidence; adherence ensures care reflects current knowledge
**Risk:** Non-adherent care may indicate quality gaps or missed opportunities

#### Questions

- [ ] Is first-line treatment consistent with guideline recommendations?
  - *Audit 1L treatment patterns against NCCN/ESMO for major indications*

- [ ] Are deviations from guidelines documented with clinical rationale?
  - *Legitimate individualization vs. unexplained variation*

- [ ] Is there a process to update practice when guidelines change?
  - *Communication, order set updates, provider education*

- [ ] Are supportive care guidelines followed (antiemetics, growth factors, etc.)?
  - *Not just treatment selection; complete care package*

- [ ] Is there monitoring of guideline-concordant care rates?
  - *Quality metric tracking with improvement targets*

### Practice: Clinical Trial Consideration

**Context:** Systematic evaluation of clinical trial eligibility for appropriate patients
**Value:** Trials provide access to innovative therapies and advance knowledge
**Risk:** Underutilization of trials limits patient options and slows progress

#### Questions

- [ ] Is clinical trial eligibility assessed for all appropriate patients?
  - *Systematic screening, not just for patients who ask*

- [ ] Is there infrastructure to identify and match patients to trials?
  - *Trial matching tools, research staff, investigator relationships*

- [ ] Are barriers to trial participation identified and addressed?
  - *Travel, time off work, caregiver burden, eligibility restrictions*

- [ ] Is trial enrollment tracked and benchmarked?
  - *Enrollment rates compared to similar institutions*

- [ ] Are patients with rare mutations or refractory disease prioritized for trials?
  - *Where standard options are limited, trial access is critical*

### Practice: Multidisciplinary Review

**Context:** Collaborative evaluation of complex cases across specialties
**Value:** MDT review improves decision quality for complex patients
**Risk:** Siloed decisions miss optimization opportunities and specialist input

#### Questions

- [ ] Are complex or high-risk cases reviewed by multidisciplinary team?
  - *Tumor board, case conference, or equivalent for appropriate patients*

- [ ] Is MDT review documented with recommendations?
  - *Not just attendance; documented discussion and recommendations*

- [ ] Are relevant specialties represented (pathology, radiology, surgery, medicine)?
  - *Complete team, not just referring and treating physicians*

- [ ] Is there patient-level tracking of MDT recommendations vs. actual treatment?
  - *Are recommendations followed? If not, why?*

- [ ] Is there a defined trigger for when MDT review is required?
  - *Not arbitrary; criteria-based identification of cases needing review*

## Domain: Treatment Sequencing Optimization

**Context:** Strategic planning of treatment order across lines of therapy
**Value:** Optimal sequencing maximizes duration of disease control and quality of life
**Risk:** Suboptimal sequencing exhausts options prematurely or misses synergy opportunities

### Practice: Line-of-Therapy Strategy

**Context:** Planned approach to treatment across first-line and subsequent therapies
**Value:** Strategic sequencing considers future options when making current decisions
**Risk:** Reactive sequencing leads to suboptimal later-line options

#### Questions

- [ ] Is there documented planning for treatment beyond first-line?
  - *Not just current treatment; discussion of anticipated sequence*

- [ ] Are later-line options considered when selecting first-line therapy?
  - *Resistance patterns, preserved options, mechanism diversity*

- [ ] Is there documentation of intended sequence at treatment initiation?
  - *Prospective plan, not just reactive decisions at progression*

- [ ] Are sequencing patterns analyzed for outcome differences?
  - *Which sequences produce better PFS, OS in practice?*

### Practice: Prior Therapy Consideration

**Context:** Accounting for previous treatments when selecting subsequent therapy
**Value:** Prior therapy exposure affects current therapy selection and expectations
**Risk:** Ignoring treatment history leads to repeated ineffective approaches

#### Questions

- [ ] Is prior therapy history systematically documented?
  - *All lines, duration, best response, reason for discontinuation*

- [ ] Are prior therapy failures analyzed for mechanism/class effects?
  - *Same-class retry vs. mechanism switch considerations*

- [ ] Is rechallenge with prior therapies considered appropriately?
  - *Treatment holidays, resensitization where supported by evidence*

- [ ] Is prior therapy status incorporated in guideline application?
  - *Many guidelines stratify by prior treatment exposure*

### Practice: Resistance Monitoring

**Context:** Identifying and responding to treatment resistance patterns
**Value:** Early recognition of resistance enables timely therapy changes
**Risk:** Delayed recognition of resistance prolongs ineffective treatment

#### Questions

- [ ] Is there defined response assessment timing?
  - *Imaging intervals, biomarker monitoring per guidelines*

- [ ] Are resistance mechanisms assessed at progression?
  - *Repeat biopsy or liquid biopsy for resistance mutations*

- [ ] Is there guidance for addressing specific resistance patterns?
  - *T790M → osimertinib; ESR1 → switch endocrine therapy*

- [ ] Is progression-free survival tracked as a quality metric?
  - *Expected vs. observed PFS to identify care improvement opportunities*

## Domain: Patient Outcomes Tracking

**Context:** Systematic monitoring of treatment effectiveness and patient wellbeing
**Value:** Outcome tracking enables quality improvement and demonstrates care quality
**Risk:** Without outcome tracking, quality problems go undetected and unaddressed

### Practice: Response Monitoring

**Context:** Assessment of treatment effectiveness through objective measures
**Value:** Timely response assessment enables therapy optimization
**Risk:** Delayed or inconsistent assessment leads to prolonged ineffective treatment

#### Questions

- [ ] Are response assessments performed at guideline-recommended intervals?
  - *8-12 week imaging for most solid tumors; per protocol intervals*

- [ ] Is response categorized using standard criteria (RECIST, PERCIST, etc.)?
  - *Consistent methodology enables tracking and comparison*

- [ ] Are response rates tracked and compared to expected benchmarks?
  - *ORR, DCR compared to clinical trial results*

- [ ] Is early progression identified and addressed promptly?
  - *Not waiting for scheduled scan when clinical progression evident*

- [ ] Is there documentation of response assessment rationale?
  - *Radiology interpretation, clinical assessment, biomarker trends*

### Practice: Quality of Life Assessment

**Context:** Patient-reported outcomes and functional status monitoring
**Value:** QoL assessment ensures care addresses patient priorities and burden
**Risk:** Focusing only on tumor response may miss treatment toxicity impact on life quality

#### Questions

- [ ] Are validated patient-reported outcome measures used?
  - *FACT, EORTC, PRO-CTCAE, or disease-specific instruments*

- [ ] Is symptom burden assessed at regular intervals?
  - *Not just when patient complains; proactive assessment*

- [ ] Are PRO results reviewed and acted upon by clinical team?
  - *Not just collected; integrated into care decisions*

- [ ] Is functional status (ECOG, KPS) documented at each visit?
  - *Performance status trends inform treatment decisions*

- [ ] Are toxicities assessed using standard grading (CTCAE)?
  - *Consistent adverse event documentation*

### Practice: Survival Metrics

**Context:** Tracking of progression-free and overall survival outcomes
**Value:** Survival outcomes are ultimate measures of treatment effectiveness
**Risk:** Without survival tracking, true care quality is unmeasured

#### Questions

- [ ] Is progression-free survival tracked by treatment line and regimen?
  - *Median PFS, PFS rates at key timepoints*

- [ ] Is overall survival tracked for major patient cohorts?
  - *OS from diagnosis, from metastatic diagnosis, from therapy initiation*

- [ ] Are survival outcomes benchmarked against clinical trial and real-world data?
  - *Expected vs. achieved outcomes*

- [ ] Is there mortality review for early or unexpected deaths?
  - *Identify potential quality improvement opportunities*

- [ ] Are survival outcomes analyzed by biomarker subgroup?
  - *Validate that precision medicine achieves expected differential outcomes*

## Domain: Health Equity & Access

**Context:** Ensuring fair and equal access to high-quality care across patient populations
**Value:** Equitable care is ethical imperative and improves population health outcomes
**Risk:** Disparities in access perpetuate health inequities and represent quality failures

### Practice: Socioeconomic Barrier Identification

**Context:** Recognition of non-clinical factors that impede optimal care
**Value:** Identifying barriers is first step to addressing them
**Risk:** Ignoring social determinants leads to care gaps and worse outcomes

#### Questions

- [ ] Is there screening for social determinants of health?
  - *Transportation, housing, food security, insurance status*

- [ ] Are SDOH factors documented in a structured format?
  - *Coded data enabling population analysis, not just free text*

- [ ] Is there analysis of treatment patterns by socioeconomic factors?
  - *Do patients with barriers receive different care?*

- [ ] Are outcomes analyzed by SDOH risk factors?
  - *Identify disparities requiring intervention*

- [ ] Is there linkage to social services for patients with identified needs?
  - *Social work referral, community resources, patient navigation*

### Practice: Access to Specialists

**Context:** Ensuring patients reach appropriate specialty care regardless of circumstances
**Value:** Specialty expertise is critical for optimal outcomes in complex diseases
**Risk:** Access barriers to specialists lead to care in less-expert settings

#### Questions

- [ ] Is time from diagnosis to specialty consultation tracked?
  - *Delays in reaching oncologist, hematologist, etc.*

- [ ] Are referral patterns analyzed for geographic or demographic variation?
  - *Rural/urban differences, racial/ethnic disparities*

- [ ] Is telemedicine available to extend specialist access?
  - *Remote consultations for patients unable to travel*

- [ ] Are there navigation services to facilitate specialty access?
  - *Patient navigators, referral coordinators*

- [ ] Is there tracking of patients lost to follow-up?
  - *Identify and address barriers to continued care*

### Practice: Support Program Utilization

**Context:** Patient use of assistance programs to enable care access
**Value:** Support programs bridge gaps between patient need and care access
**Risk:** Underutilization of available support leaves barriers unaddressed

#### Questions

- [ ] Are eligible patients connected with financial assistance programs?
  - *Copay assistance, patient assistance, foundation support*

- [ ] Is there tracking of program enrollment and utilization rates?
  - *Are programs reaching intended beneficiaries?*

- [ ] Is there proactive identification of patients at risk for non-adherence?
  - *Cost, complexity, side effects as risk factors*

- [ ] Are transportation and lodging barriers addressed?
  - *Hospital-provided transport, accommodation assistance*

- [ ] Is there analysis of treatment completion rates by patient demographic?
  - *Identify populations with higher dropout or discontinuation*

### Practice: Diversity in Clinical Trials

**Context:** Representation of diverse populations in research participation
**Value:** Diverse trials produce generalizable evidence applicable to all patients
**Risk:** Homogeneous trials generate evidence that may not apply to underrepresented groups

#### Questions

- [ ] Are trial enrollment demographics tracked and reported?
  - *Race, ethnicity, age, socioeconomic status representation*

- [ ] Is there active outreach to increase diverse trial participation?
  - *Community engagement, translated materials, site selection*

- [ ] Are trial eligibility criteria reviewed for unnecessary exclusions?
  - *Comorbidity restrictions, age limits, performance status thresholds*

- [ ] Is there analysis of trial participation barriers by demographic?
  - *Why do certain groups enroll at lower rates?*

- [ ] Are trial results analyzed by demographic subgroup when possible?
  - *Identify differential efficacy or safety by population*
