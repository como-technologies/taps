# Assessment: Pharmaceutical Market Research Program Maturity

> Evaluate the capabilities and practices of a pharmaceutical company's market research function to ensure rigorous, actionable, and compliant research outputs.

**Goal:** Build a market research program that generates reliable insights, informs strategic decisions, and maintains regulatory compliance across global markets.

---

## Domain: Survey Design & Methodology

**Context:** How research instruments are constructed to capture valid, unbiased data from healthcare professionals and patients
**Value:** Well-designed surveys yield reliable insights, reduce respondent burden, and support defensible business decisions
**Risk:** Poor methodology produces misleading data, wastes budget, and can lead to flawed commercial or clinical strategies

### Practice: Question Quality

**Context:** Individual survey questions that are clear, unbiased, and collect actionable data
**Value:** High-quality questions reduce respondent confusion, improve completion rates, and produce analyzable responses
**Risk:** Ambiguous or leading questions generate unreliable data that cannot support decision-making

#### Questions

- [ ] Are survey questions free of leading or biased phrasing?
  - *Review for assumptions embedded in questions; use neutral language*

- [ ] Do percentage-based questions enforce sum-to-100 validation?
  - *Treatment distribution, patient mix, and time allocation questions should validate totals*

- [ ] Are response options mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive?
  - *Check for overlapping categories or missing options; include "Other" where appropriate*

- [ ] Are screener questions validated against target population criteria?
  - *Verify qualification logic matches study objectives; test edge cases*

- [ ] Do questions use consistent terminology throughout the survey?
  - *Drug names, disease states, and treatment lines should use standardized terms*

- [ ] Are questions localized appropriately for international markets?
  - *Beyond translation: verify cultural relevance, local treatment patterns, regulatory terms*

### Practice: Sampling Strategy

**Context:** Methods for selecting and recruiting representative respondents
**Value:** Proper sampling ensures findings generalize to target populations and support confident extrapolation
**Risk:** Biased samples produce misleading conclusions that don't reflect actual market conditions

#### Questions

- [ ] Is the target respondent profile clearly defined with inclusion/exclusion criteria?
  - *Document specialty, practice setting, patient volume, geographic requirements*

- [ ] Are multiple recruitment channels used to reduce selection bias?
  - *Panel, HCP networks, professional associations, direct outreach*

- [ ] Is the sample size sufficient for planned subgroup analyses?
  - *Power calculations for key segments: specialty, region, practice type*

- [ ] Are quotas established to ensure representation across key segments?
  - *Geographic distribution, specialty mix, practice size balance*

- [ ] Is there a process to identify and exclude professional survey-takers?
  - *Quality checks, trap questions, response time analysis*

### Practice: Bias Prevention

**Context:** Systematic approaches to minimize cognitive and selection biases in research
**Value:** Unbiased research provides accurate market understanding and supports sound strategy
**Risk:** Unrecognized biases lead to overconfidence in flawed insights and poor business decisions

#### Questions

- [ ] Are questions randomized where order effects could influence responses?
  - *Randomize treatment lists, attribute rankings, brand awareness sequences*

- [ ] Is the survey sponsor identity masked when appropriate?
  - *Blind sponsorship for competitive assessment and unbiased perceptions*

- [ ] Are recall periods appropriate for the information being collected?
  - *Recent behavior (3-6 months) more reliable than distant recall (>1 year)*

- [ ] Are social desirability biases mitigated in sensitive questions?
  - *Normalize ranges, use indirect questioning for guideline adherence*

- [ ] Is there pre-testing with cognitive debriefing?
  - *Test survey with representative respondents to identify confusing elements*

## Domain: Data Collection & Quality

**Context:** How survey data is captured, validated, and stored to ensure accuracy and compliance
**Value:** High-quality data collection enables confident analysis and supports regulatory and ethical requirements
**Risk:** Data quality issues invalidate findings, waste investment, and may create compliance exposure

### Practice: Multi-Channel Fielding

**Context:** Deploying surveys across appropriate platforms and managing respondent experience
**Value:** Effective fielding maximizes response rates, reduces bias, and respects respondent time
**Risk:** Poor fielding execution yields low response rates, incomplete data, and unrepresentative samples

#### Questions

- [ ] Is the survey platform validated for the target device types (desktop, mobile, tablet)?
  - *Test rendering, skip logic, and validation on all expected devices*

- [ ] Are estimated completion times accurate and communicated upfront?
  - *Set expectations; long surveys need staged incentives or breaks*

- [ ] Is progress indication provided to reduce abandonment?
  - *Progress bar or section indicators improve completion rates*

- [ ] Are reminder protocols defined for incomplete responses?
  - *Timing, frequency, and channel for follow-up communications*

- [ ] Is there real-time monitoring of field performance?
  - *Track quota fills, completion rates, drop-off points during fielding*

### Practice: Response Validation

**Context:** Quality checks to identify and address problematic responses
**Value:** Data validation ensures analysis is based on genuine, thoughtful responses
**Risk:** Invalid responses contaminate data and lead to incorrect conclusions

#### Questions

- [ ] Are speeder checks implemented (minimum response time thresholds)?
  - *Flag responses completed faster than reading time allows*

- [ ] Are straightlining patterns detected in grid questions?
  - *Identify respondents selecting same answer across all items*

- [ ] Is open-end response quality reviewed?
  - *Check for gibberish, copy-paste, or responses inconsistent with closed-ends*

- [ ] Are logical consistency checks enforced?
  - *Flag contradictory responses (e.g., no patients treated but detailed treatment patterns)*

- [ ] Is there a process for handling and documenting exclusions?
  - *Clear criteria, audit trail, impact assessment on sample*

### Practice: Privacy & Compliance

**Context:** Adherence to data protection regulations and ethical research standards
**Value:** Compliance protects respondents, company reputation, and enables global research operations
**Risk:** Non-compliance results in regulatory penalties, research restrictions, and reputational damage

#### Questions

- [ ] Is informed consent obtained with clear explanation of data use?
  - *GDPR, HIPAA considerations; right to withdraw documented*

- [ ] Is respondent data anonymized or pseudonymized appropriately?
  - *Personal identifiers separated from response data; aggregation thresholds*

- [ ] Are data retention policies defined and enforced?
  - *Clear timelines, secure deletion processes, documentation*

- [ ] Is data transfer compliant with cross-border requirements?
  - *Standard contractual clauses, adequacy decisions for international studies*

- [ ] Are vendor and panel partner agreements reviewed for compliance?
  - *DPAs in place, audit rights, breach notification procedures*

## Domain: Analysis & Synthesis

**Context:** How collected data is processed, analyzed, and transformed into insights
**Value:** Rigorous analysis converts raw data into reliable, actionable intelligence
**Risk:** Flawed analysis leads to incorrect conclusions and misinformed business decisions

### Practice: Statistical Rigor

**Context:** Application of appropriate statistical methods to survey data
**Value:** Proper statistical treatment ensures findings are reliable and appropriately qualified
**Risk:** Inappropriate methods overstate confidence or miss genuine patterns in data

#### Questions

- [ ] Are statistical tests appropriate for sample size and data type?
  - *Verify assumptions; use non-parametric tests when distributions warrant*

- [ ] Are confidence intervals and significance levels reported?
  - *Don't report point estimates without uncertainty bounds*

- [ ] Is weighting applied when sample deviates from target population?
  - *Document weighting scheme; assess impact on key findings*

- [ ] Are subgroup analyses qualified with appropriate sample size caveats?
  - *Small cells clearly flagged; avoid over-interpretation*

- [ ] Is multiple testing addressed in comparative analyses?
  - *Bonferroni or similar corrections when running multiple comparisons*

### Practice: Cross-Sectional Analysis

**Context:** Examining patterns across respondent segments and data dimensions
**Value:** Cross-sectional views reveal important differences across markets, specialties, and patient types
**Risk:** Missing segment differences leads to undifferentiated strategy that fails in key markets

#### Questions

- [ ] Are key subgroup comparisons pre-specified in the analysis plan?
  - *Define primary cuts: specialty, region, practice type, patient volume*

- [ ] Are differences tested for statistical significance?
  - *Report whether observed differences exceed sampling error*

- [ ] Are interaction effects explored for important variables?
  - *Does the effect of X differ by level of Y?*

- [ ] Is data stratified by treatment line where relevant?
  - *1L, 2L, 3L+ patterns often differ substantially*

- [ ] Are socioeconomic factors incorporated where available?
  - *SDOH variables: access, insurance status, geographic factors*

### Practice: Longitudinal Tracking

**Context:** Monitoring trends and changes over time through repeated measurement
**Value:** Trend data reveals market dynamics, campaign effectiveness, and emerging opportunities
**Risk:** Single-point-in-time data misses critical shifts and fails to demonstrate impact

#### Questions

- [ ] Are tracker studies designed with consistent methodology across waves?
  - *Same questions, sample specs, timing to enable valid comparisons*

- [ ] Are year-over-year and wave-over-wave trends analyzed?
  - *Calculate change scores, test significance of movement*

- [ ] Is respondent overlap managed in tracker panels?
  - *Fresh sample vs. longitudinal cohort trade-offs documented*

- [ ] Are trend breaks documented when methodology changes?
  - *Flag non-comparable waves; provide bridging analysis if possible*

- [ ] Is seasonality considered in fielding and interpretation?
  - *Treatment patterns, congress timing, budget cycles may create artifacts*

## Domain: Insights Translation

**Context:** How analytical findings are communicated and applied to business decisions
**Value:** Effective translation ensures research investment yields strategic impact
**Risk:** Good research that isn't actioned wastes investment and perpetuates decision-making by intuition

### Practice: Stakeholder Communication

**Context:** Presenting findings to diverse internal and external audiences
**Value:** Clear communication builds credibility, enables action, and demonstrates research value
**Risk:** Poor communication leads to misinterpretation, disengagement, and underutilization of insights

#### Questions

- [ ] Are executive summaries provided with key findings and implications?
  - *One-page overview for senior leadership; detailed appendices for analysts*

- [ ] Are visualizations clear, accurate, and appropriate for the data?
  - *Avoid misleading scales, 3D effects, or overcrowded charts*

- [ ] Are limitations and caveats clearly stated?
  - *Sample size, methodology constraints, generalizability boundaries*

- [ ] Are findings presented with competitive context where relevant?
  - *Position results against market benchmarks or prior waves*

- [ ] Is there a structured forum for stakeholder Q&A and discussion?
  - *Readout sessions, office hours, or interactive dashboards*

### Practice: Actionable Recommendations

**Context:** Converting insights into specific, implementable guidance
**Value:** Clear recommendations bridge the gap between data and action
**Risk:** Insights without recommendations leave stakeholders uncertain about next steps

#### Questions

- [ ] Do recommendations specify who should act and what they should do?
  - *"Commercial team should..." not just "Consider improving..."*

- [ ] Are recommendations prioritized by impact and feasibility?
  - *High/medium/low or similar framework for triage*

- [ ] Are recommendations linked to specific data findings?
  - *Traceability from insight to action; avoid unsupported opinions*

- [ ] Are alternative interpretations and counter-arguments addressed?
  - *Acknowledge uncertainty; present scenarios where appropriate*

- [ ] Is there follow-up to assess recommendation implementation?
  - *Track which recommendations were actioned; measure outcomes*

### Practice: Strategic Alignment

**Context:** Ensuring research program serves organizational strategy and decision needs
**Value:** Aligned research maximizes ROI and positions market research as strategic partner
**Risk:** Misaligned research produces interesting but unused insights; erodes credibility

#### Questions

- [ ] Is there a research roadmap aligned with brand planning cycles?
  - *Studies timed to inform key decisions; avoid orphan research*

- [ ] Are research objectives derived from explicit business questions?
  - *Start with decision to be made, not methodology preference*

- [ ] Is there a knowledge management system for accessing prior research?
  - *Searchable repository prevents redundant studies; enables synthesis*

- [ ] Are research findings integrated into brand planning processes?
  - *Formal input to strategic reviews, forecasts, competitive assessments*

- [ ] Is the research function measured on business impact, not just activity?
  - *Track decisions influenced, not just studies completed*
