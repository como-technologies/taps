# Assessment: Competitive Position Tracker

> Evaluate a pharmaceutical product's competitive position and market dynamics through quarterly assessment, with built-in support for evolving metrics over time.

**Goal:** Track competitive position trends, identify share movement drivers, and detect early signals requiring strategic response.

**Assessment Cadence:** Quarterly (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4)

---

## Longitudinal Assessment Design

### Handling Assessment Evolution

This assessment is designed for quarterly repetition with the expectation that questions will evolve over time. The framework handles this through:

**Question Stability Tiers:**

| Tier | Description | Change Frequency | Trending |
|------|-------------|------------------|----------|
| **Core** | Fundamental competitive metrics | Rarely (annual review) | Full history |
| **Strategic** | Current strategic priorities | Quarterly review | Rolling 4Q |
| **Exploratory** | Emerging issues, one-time deep dives | As needed | Point-in-time |

**When Questions Change:**
- **New questions**: Mark as "New in Qn" - no prior trend, baseline established
- **Modified questions**: Document change, note trend break, provide bridging guidance
- **Retired questions**: Archive with final reading, document reason for retirement
- **Split questions**: Link to parent question for historical context

**Trend Visualization Approach:**
- Core metrics: Show full available history
- Strategic metrics: Show rolling 4 quarters with notation of any methodology changes
- Exploratory: Show single quarter or comparison to specific prior quarter

## Domain: Market Share Position

**Context:** Quantitative share of prescriptions, patients, or revenue in the target market
**Value:** Share position is the fundamental measure of competitive success
**Risk:** Share decline signals competitive vulnerability and threatens commercial viability

### Practice: Overall Share Tracking [CORE]

**Context:** Total market share across all segments and channels
**Value:** Headline metric for competitive health and commercial performance
**Risk:** Declining share triggers revenue loss and may indicate fundamental positioning problems

#### Questions

- [ ] Is overall market share at or above target for this quarter?
  - *Compare to annual plan target; document variance*
  - *Trend: [Q-4] [Q-3] [Q-2] [Q-1] [Current]*

- [ ] Has market share increased versus prior quarter?
  - *Calculate Q/Q change; note if within normal variance*

- [ ] Has market share increased versus same quarter prior year?
  - *Y/Y comparison controls for seasonality*

- [ ] Is share trajectory on track for annual target?
  - *Project current trend; flag if remediation needed*

- [ ] Is share performance consistent with promotional investment level?
  - *ROI check: share gain per promotional dollar*

### Practice: Segment Share Analysis [CORE]

**Context:** Share position within key market segments (treatment line, biomarker, setting)
**Value:** Segment-level view reveals where product is winning/losing and why
**Risk:** Hidden segment weakness may mask overall share problems until too late

#### Questions

- [ ] Is first-line market share at or above segment target?
  - *1L share often highest priority; track separately*
  - *Trend: [Q-4] [Q-3] [Q-2] [Q-1] [Current]*

- [ ] Is second-line market share at or above segment target?
  - *2L may show different dynamics than 1L*

- [ ] Is third-line+ market share at or above segment target?
  - *Later lines often more competitive; differentiation critical*

- [ ] Is share in biomarker-positive patients at or above target?
  - *Precision medicine segment often strategic priority*

- [ ] Is share in biomarker-negative patients at or above target?
  - *Broader population share; different competitive set*

- [ ] Are there segments with share decline >2 points Q/Q?
  - *Flag segments needing immediate attention*

### Practice: New Patient Share [STRATEGIC]

**Context:** Share of newly initiated patients (vs. continuing patients)
**Value:** New patient share is leading indicator of future total share
**Risk:** Losing new patient share predicts future total share decline

#### Questions

- [ ] Is new patient share higher than total patient share?
  - *Growing products have NRx share > TRx share*

- [ ] Is new patient share increasing Q/Q?
  - *Trend direction more important than absolute level*

- [ ] Is time-to-first-prescription after diagnosis improving?
  - *Earlier capture = more patient months*

- [ ] Are switchers-in exceeding switchers-out?
  - *Net switch balance indicates momentum direction*

## Domain: Competitive Dynamics

**Context:** Relative position versus specific competitors and competitive response patterns
**Value:** Understanding competitive dynamics enables proactive strategy adjustment
**Risk:** Being surprised by competitive moves leads to reactive, ineffective responses

### Practice: Head-to-Head Tracking [CORE]

**Context:** Direct share comparison with primary competitors
**Value:** Relative position versus key competitors is often more important than absolute share
**Risk:** Losing ground to specific competitors may require targeted response

#### Questions

- [ ] Is share gap versus Competitor A stable or improving?
  - *Track gap, not just own share; competitor gaining faster is warning sign*
  - *Trend: [Q-4] [Q-3] [Q-2] [Q-1] [Current]*

- [ ] Is share gap versus Competitor B stable or improving?
  - *Track each major competitor separately*

- [ ] In head-to-head switches, is net switch balance positive?
  - *Of patients switching between our product and Competitor A, are we net gaining?*

- [ ] Is win rate in new patient contests stable or improving?
  - *When HCP choosing between us and competitor for new patient, who wins?*

### Practice: Competitive Activity Monitoring [STRATEGIC]

**Context:** Tracking competitor promotional and strategic actions
**Value:** Early detection of competitive moves enables timely response
**Risk:** Delayed awareness of competitive activity leads to share loss before response

#### Questions

- [ ] Have competitors launched new promotional campaigns this quarter?
  - *New messages, increased spending, new channels*

- [ ] Have competitors announced new clinical data or indications?
  - *Label changes, new trial results, guideline updates*

- [ ] Have competitors made significant pricing or access changes?
  - *Contract changes, copay programs, rebate adjustments*

- [ ] Are there new market entrants or pending approvals to monitor?
  - *Pipeline products approaching launch; biosimilar/generic threats*

- [ ] Has competitor share of voice changed significantly?
  - *Detail levels, speaker programs, digital presence*

### Practice: Prescriber Behavior Shifts [STRATEGIC]

**Context:** Changes in how physicians make treatment decisions
**Value:** Understanding prescriber behavior changes enables message and targeting adjustment
**Risk:** Missing prescriber behavior shifts leads to misaligned commercial approach

#### Questions

- [ ] Has the primary decision driver changed for target prescribers?
  - *Efficacy vs. safety vs. convenience vs. access - which is top?*

- [ ] Are prescribers citing different reasons for competitive preference?
  - *Track reasons for choosing competitor; enables response*

- [ ] Has biomarker testing behavior changed significantly?
  - *Testing rates, timing, result utilization*

- [ ] Are treatment sequencing patterns shifting?
  - *What's used in 1L affects 2L/3L opportunity*

## Domain: Access & Pricing Position

**Context:** Formulary status, coverage, and net pricing across payer segments
**Value:** Access position determines whether prescriptions can convert to revenue
**Risk:** Access erosion directly impacts revenue realization and prescriber willingness

### Practice: Formulary Position Tracking [CORE]

**Context:** Coverage status across commercial, Medicare, and Medicaid payers
**Value:** Formulary position determines patient access and prescriber convenience
**Risk:** Formulary losses create immediate revenue impact and prescriber switching

#### Questions

- [ ] Has formulary coverage been maintained across major commercial payers?
  - *No losses to non-preferred status or step edits*
  - *Trend: Track # of lives with preferred access Q/Q*

- [ ] Has Medicare Part D coverage been maintained?
  - *LIS status, tier position, utilization management*

- [ ] Has Medicaid coverage been maintained in key states?
  - *PDL status, PA requirements by state*

- [ ] Have any new access restrictions been imposed this quarter?
  - *New step edits, PA criteria, quantity limits*

- [ ] Is percentage of prescriptions with unrestricted access stable?
  - *Track % Rx requiring PA or step therapy*

### Practice: Net Price Realization [CORE]

**Context:** Actual revenue per prescription after rebates and discounts
**Value:** Net price is true revenue; gross price is accounting fiction
**Risk:** Net price erosion destroys profitability even with stable volume

#### Questions

- [ ] Is net price per prescription at or above target?
  - *Gross-to-net, realized price after all deductions*

- [ ] Is net price stable or improving versus prior quarter?
  - *Q/Q trend; flag accelerating erosion*

- [ ] Is gross-to-net ratio stable?
  - *GTN expansion signals pricing pressure*

- [ ] Are contract renewals maintaining price position?
  - *New contracts at equivalent or better terms*

### Practice: Patient Affordability [STRATEGIC]

**Context:** Out-of-pocket cost burden and support program utilization
**Value:** Affordability barriers prevent prescriptions from filling; support programs bridge gaps
**Risk:** High OOP costs lead to abandonment and damage HCP confidence in product

#### Questions

- [ ] Is prescription abandonment rate at or below target?
  - *% of Rx written but never filled*

- [ ] Is copay assistance utilization meeting expectations?
  - *Enrollment rate, redemption rate, cost per claim*

- [ ] Are patients reporting affordability concerns in market research?
  - *Track patient-reported barriers*

- [ ] Is average patient OOP cost competitive with alternatives?
  - *Benchmark against competitor OOP*

## Domain: Prescriber Engagement

**Context:** Reach, frequency, and quality of engagement with target healthcare professionals
**Value:** Prescriber engagement drives awareness, preference, and prescription behavior
**Risk:** Inadequate engagement leads to share loss to more visible competitors

### Practice: Reach & Frequency [CORE]

**Context:** Percentage of target HCPs contacted and contact frequency
**Value:** Fundamental measure of commercial execution
**Risk:** Under-reaching target HCPs leaves opportunity for competitors

#### Questions

- [ ] Is target HCP reach at or above quarterly goal?
  - *% of targets contacted at least once*
  - *Trend: [Q-4] [Q-3] [Q-2] [Q-1] [Current]*

- [ ] Is average call frequency at or above plan?
  - *Calls per target per quarter*

- [ ] Is high-decile prescriber coverage meeting higher frequency targets?
  - *Top targets should get more attention*

- [ ] Is non-personal promotion reaching HCPs not covered by field force?
  - *Digital, peer-to-peer, medical for non-target HCPs*

### Practice: Message Delivery [STRATEGIC]

**Context:** Consistency and effectiveness of promotional messaging
**Value:** Right messages delivered effectively drive preference
**Risk:** Off-message or poorly delivered content wastes promotional investment

#### Questions

- [ ] Are core messages being delivered consistently per field reports?
  - *Message tracking from CRM data or manager observation*

- [ ] Is message recall in market research aligned with intended positioning?
  - *HCPs hearing what we intend them to hear*

- [ ] Are competitive counter-messages effective?
  - *When competitor advantage raised, can reps respond effectively?*

- [ ] Is new data/evidence being integrated into details?
  - *Recent publications, real-world evidence incorporated*

### Practice: HCP Perception Tracking [STRATEGIC]

**Context:** Prescriber attitudes, perceptions, and preference for product
**Value:** Perceptions drive behavior; tracking enables early intervention
**Risk:** Perception problems lead to behavior change if not addressed

#### Questions

- [ ] Is unaided awareness stable or improving?
  - *Brand mention without prompting*

- [ ] Is preference share stable or improving?
  - *% of HCPs naming product as preferred in category*

- [ ] Are key attribute ratings (efficacy, safety, convenience) stable?
  - *Track ratings on critical decision drivers*

- [ ] Is prescriber confidence in product stable?
  - *Overall confidence measure*

## Domain: Leading Indicators

**Context:** Early signals that may predict future share movement
**Value:** Leading indicators enable proactive intervention before share impact
**Risk:** Ignoring early signals leads to delayed response and larger share losses

### Practice: Pipeline Signals [STRATEGIC]

**Context:** Indicators of future prescription volume
**Value:** Forward-looking metrics predict future performance
**Risk:** Lagging indicators only show problems after they've occurred

#### Questions

- [ ] Is sample utilization rate meeting expectations?
  - *Samples requested/dispensed as % of target*

- [ ] Are patient start forms trending at or above target?
  - *Leading indicator of new prescriptions*

- [ ] Is hub enrollment trending at or above target?
  - *Patients entering support programs*

- [ ] Are prior authorization approvals maintaining historical rates?
  - *Approval rate and time-to-approval*

### Practice: Digital Engagement Signals [EXPLORATORY]

**Context:** Online and digital behavior of HCPs related to product
**Value:** Digital behavior often precedes prescription behavior
**Risk:** Missing digital signals leaves blind spots in engagement picture

#### Questions

- [ ] Is website traffic to product pages stable or growing?
  - *HCP portal visits, content engagement*

- [ ] Is engagement with digital content (email, banner) meeting benchmarks?
  - *Open rates, click rates, time on content*

- [ ] Is social media sentiment neutral or positive?
  - *HCP social listening for product mentions*

- [ ] Are medical information inquiries trending normally?
  - *Spikes may indicate issues or opportunities*

### Practice: Quality & Safety Signals [CORE]

**Context:** Adverse event reports, complaints, and quality issues
**Value:** Safety signals can rapidly impact prescribing if not managed
**Risk:** Undetected safety signals can lead to sudden share collapse

#### Questions

- [ ] Are adverse event reports within expected ranges?
  - *AE rate vs. historical baseline and label expectations*

- [ ] Are there any new safety signals requiring investigation?
  - *Pharmacovigilance flags, unexpected AE patterns*

- [ ] Is product complaint rate within normal parameters?
  - *Quality complaints, packaging issues, supply problems*

- [ ] Have any regulatory safety communications been required?
  - *Dear Doctor letters, label updates, REMS changes*

## Assessment Administration

### Quarterly Execution Checklist

- [ ] Schedule data collection 2 weeks before quarter end
- [ ] Confirm data sources are updated (Rx data, market research, CRM)
- [ ] Review any question modifications needed for this quarter
- [ ] Document any market events affecting interpretation (new entrants, recalls, etc.)
- [ ] Compare results to prior quarter and flag significant changes
- [ ] Prepare trend visualizations for stakeholder review
- [ ] Identify items requiring immediate action vs. monitoring
- [ ] Archive completed assessment with all supporting data

### Question Modification Log

| Quarter | Change Type | Question | Rationale |
|---------|-------------|----------|-----------|
| Q1 2024 | Baseline | All | Initial assessment deployment |
| Q2 2024 | Add | [Example: New competitor share] | Competitor B launched |
| Q2 2024 | Modify | [Example: Segment definition] | Biomarker+ split into BRCA+ and other |
| Q3 2024 | Retire | [Example: Legacy metric] | No longer strategically relevant |

### Trend Break Documentation

When methodology changes prevent direct trending:
1. Document the change and rationale
2. Provide last reading under old methodology
3. Provide first reading under new methodology
4. Note that trend line has a break at this point
5. If possible, provide bridging estimate (e.g., "new method reads ~3 points higher")
