# Assessment: Commercial Launch Readiness

> Evaluate a pharmaceutical commercial team's preparedness to successfully launch a new product in the market.

**Goal:** Ensure all commercial capabilities, market intelligence, and organizational readiness are in place to maximize launch impact and achieve revenue objectives.

---

## Domain: Market Understanding

**Context:** Depth and accuracy of knowledge about the therapeutic landscape, patient journey, and market dynamics
**Value:** Deep market understanding enables effective positioning, messaging, and resource allocation
**Risk:** Poor market understanding leads to mispositioned products, missed opportunities, and competitive vulnerability

### Practice: Patient Journey Mapping

**Context:** Comprehensive understanding of how patients move through diagnosis, treatment, and outcomes
**Value:** Patient journey insights inform touchpoints, messaging, and support program design
**Risk:** Blind spots in patient journey create missed intervention opportunities and suboptimal patient experience

#### Questions

- [ ] Has a detailed patient journey been mapped from symptoms to long-term outcomes?
  - *Document key stages, decision points, and time intervals*

- [ ] Are patient drop-off and delay points identified?
  - *Where patients fall out of care or experience treatment gaps*

- [ ] Have patient unmet needs been validated through primary research?
  - *Not assumed; confirmed through patient interviews or surveys*

- [ ] Is the diagnostic pathway understood, including typical time to diagnosis?
  - *Average time from symptoms to confirmed diagnosis; variation factors*

- [ ] Are key influencers in treatment decisions identified (beyond physicians)?
  - *Caregivers, patient advocacy groups, online communities, second opinions*

### Practice: Treatment Landscape Analysis

**Context:** Understanding of current and emerging treatment options in the therapeutic area
**Value:** Clear landscape view enables differentiated positioning and competitive strategy
**Risk:** Incomplete landscape understanding leads to blind spots and competitive surprises

#### Questions

- [ ] Is the current standard of care clearly defined by treatment line?
  - *1L, 2L, 3L+ standards documented with supporting evidence*

- [ ] Are treatment sequencing patterns documented from market research?
  - *How physicians actually treat, not just guidelines*

- [ ] Are key pipeline products identified with expected launch timelines?
  - *Competitive intelligence on phase 3 and filed products*

- [ ] Are biosimilar/generic threats assessed with timeline and impact estimates?
  - *Patent expirations, ANDA filings, expected erosion curves*

- [ ] Is combination therapy landscape understood?
  - *How product fits with other agents; emerging combinations*

### Practice: Unmet Need Identification

**Context:** Gaps in current treatment options that the product can address
**Value:** Clear unmet need articulation enables compelling value proposition and stakeholder engagement
**Risk:** Vague or unsupported unmet need claims undermine credibility and market access

#### Questions

- [ ] Are unmet needs quantified, not just qualitatively described?
  - *Percentage of patients affected, magnitude of gap, clinical impact*

- [ ] Is there primary research validating perceived unmet needs?
  - *HCP and patient research confirming need priority and relevance*

- [ ] Is the product's ability to address unmet needs supported by clinical data?
  - *Claims linked to trial endpoints; not just positioning aspiration*

- [ ] Are unmet needs differentiated by patient segment?
  - *Different needs for 1L vs. later line, biomarker subgroups, etc.*

## Domain: Physician Segmentation & Targeting

**Context:** Strategy for identifying, prioritizing, and engaging healthcare professionals
**Value:** Efficient targeting maximizes ROI on commercial investment and accelerates adoption
**Risk:** Poor targeting wastes resources on low-value HCPs while under-serving high-potential prescribers

### Practice: KOL Identification

**Context:** Identifying and engaging key opinion leaders who influence prescribing behavior
**Value:** KOL engagement shapes treatment paradigms and accelerates adoption
**Risk:** Missing key influencers or over-relying on accessible but non-influential HCPs

#### Questions

- [ ] Is KOL identification based on objective criteria (publications, trials, speaking)?
  - *Not just physician preference or sales relationships*

- [ ] Are digital opinion leaders (DOLs) identified alongside traditional KOLs?
  - *Social media, podcast, online education influencers*

- [ ] Is there a tiered KOL engagement strategy?
  - *National, regional, local tiers with appropriate engagement levels*

- [ ] Are KOL relationships tracked with engagement history?
  - *CRM documentation of interactions, advisory board participation, etc.*

- [ ] Is there a process for identifying emerging leaders?
  - *Rising stars, fellows, new faculty with growing influence*

### Practice: Prescriber Tiering

**Context:** Segmentation of target prescribers by volume and influence for resource allocation
**Value:** Tiered targeting optimizes field force deployment and resource allocation
**Risk:** Flat targeting wastes resources or under-invests in highest-value segments

#### Questions

- [ ] Are prescribers segmented by current and potential prescribing volume?
  - *Deciles, quintiles, or similar volume-based tiering*

- [ ] Does tiering incorporate behavioral and attitudinal factors?
  - *Early adopter vs. laggard; guideline-driven vs. experience-driven*

- [ ] Are targeting lists updated with appropriate frequency?
  - *At least quarterly refresh; not stale lists from launch*

- [ ] Is there alignment between targeting tiers and call plan frequency?
  - *Higher tiers get more interactions; appropriate channel mix*

- [ ] Are institutional prescribers appropriately identified?
  - *Academic centers, large group practices, health systems*

### Practice: Specialty Focus

**Context:** Understanding which specialties prescribe and influence prescribing
**Value:** Appropriate specialty targeting ensures reach to relevant decision-makers
**Risk:** Over-focus on one specialty misses prescribing from other relevant specialties

#### Questions

- [ ] Are all prescribing specialties identified with volume estimates?
  - *Primary specialty plus adjacent specialists who prescribe*

- [ ] Is the referral pathway between specialties understood?
  - *Who diagnoses, who treats, who influences treatment selection*

- [ ] Are specialty-specific messaging and engagement approaches developed?
  - *Different value propositions for different specialty perspectives*

- [ ] Are non-physician prescribers (NPs, PAs) appropriately targeted?
  - *Growing prescriber segment in many therapeutic areas*

## Domain: Competitive Intelligence

**Context:** Systematic understanding of competitive products, strategies, and market dynamics
**Value:** Competitive intelligence enables differentiated positioning and proactive response
**Risk:** Competitive blind spots lead to market share loss and reactive strategy

### Practice: Share of Voice Monitoring

**Context:** Tracking promotional presence and messaging across competitors
**Value:** Share of voice insights inform promotional investment and message evolution
**Risk:** Losing share of voice to competitors without awareness or response

#### Questions

- [ ] Is competitive promotional spending tracked?
  - *DDD/IMS data on detail levels, samples, speaker programs*

- [ ] Are competitive messages monitored and catalogued?
  - *Core messages, visual aids, approved claims*

- [ ] Is digital/social competitive activity monitored?
  - *Paid media, organic social, digital content*

- [ ] Are competitive medical education activities tracked?
  - *CME, symposia, advisory boards, publications*

- [ ] Is there a systematic process for sales intelligence on competitive activity?
  - *Field reporting, win/loss analysis*

### Practice: Competitive Positioning

**Context:** Clear differentiation strategy versus key competitors
**Value:** Strong positioning enables preference and formulary access
**Risk:** Me-too positioning fails to drive switching or preference

#### Questions

- [ ] Are key competitive differentiators identified and prioritized?
  - *Efficacy, safety, convenience, access; rank ordered*

- [ ] Is the competitive positioning defensible with approved claims?
  - *Not just aspiration; supported by data and compliant messaging*

- [ ] Are competitive weaknesses identified for appropriate contrast?
  - *Competitor limitations that create opportunity*

- [ ] Is the positioning tested with target HCPs?
  - *Message testing research to validate resonance*

### Practice: Differentiation Strategy

**Context:** Sustainable competitive advantage in the market
**Value:** Clear differentiation drives adoption and reduces price sensitivity
**Risk:** Commodity positioning leads to access/price competition

#### Questions

- [ ] Is there a clear answer to "why should I switch/choose this product?"
  - *Compelling, simple, differentiated value proposition*

- [ ] Is differentiation relevant to target HCP decision drivers?
  - *Addresses what they care about, not just what's different*

- [ ] Is differentiation sustainable vs. expected competitive responses?
  - *Defensible over time; not easily neutralized*

- [ ] Are proof points available for differentiation claims?
  - *Clinical data, real-world evidence, economic data*

## Domain: Access & Reimbursement

**Context:** Strategy for securing formulary access and patient affordability
**Value:** Access success is essential for commercial viability; pricing and coverage drive revenue
**Risk:** Access failures limit prescribing, patient access, and revenue realization

### Practice: Payer Landscape Analysis

**Context:** Understanding of payer mix, coverage patterns, and decision-making
**Value:** Payer landscape knowledge enables targeted access strategy and realistic forecasting
**Risk:** Payer blind spots lead to access surprises and unrealistic revenue expectations

#### Questions

- [ ] Is the payer mix documented by lives covered?
  - *Commercial, Medicare, Medicaid, managed Medicaid proportions*

- [ ] Are key payer decision-makers and processes identified?
  - *P&T committees, medical directors, timing of reviews*

- [ ] Is competitor access status documented across major payers?
  - *Formulary position, step edits, PA requirements for competitors*

- [ ] Are specialty pharmacy networks relevant and understood?
  - *If specialty product: which SPs, network requirements*

- [ ] Is site-of-care impact on reimbursement assessed?
  - *Buy-and-bill vs. specialty pharmacy; 340B implications*

### Practice: Coverage Strategy

**Context:** Plan for achieving optimal formulary position across payers
**Value:** Effective coverage strategy ensures patient access and revenue realization
**Risk:** Inadequate access strategy leads to tier position losses and market share erosion

#### Questions

- [ ] Is there a target formulary position by payer segment?
  - *Preferred brand, parity, access with PA; goals by payer type*

- [ ] Is the value dossier complete and payer-tested?
  - *Economic, clinical, and humanistic value evidence compiled*

- [ ] Are health economic models validated and credible?
  - *ICER review, payer research validation*

- [ ] Is there a contracting strategy with defined parameters?
  - *Rebate levels, outcomes-based options, net price targets*

- [ ] Are access timelines realistic in the launch plan?
  - *Coverage decisions typically 6-12 months post-launch*

### Practice: Patient Support Programs

**Context:** Programs to assist patients with access, affordability, and adherence
**Value:** Effective patient support improves access, compliance, and outcomes
**Risk:** Weak patient support leads to abandonment, non-compliance, and poor outcomes

#### Questions

- [ ] Is a copay assistance program in place for commercially insured patients?
  - *Card program, hub enrollment, financial assistance criteria*

- [ ] Is there a patient assistance program for uninsured/underinsured?
  - *Free drug program, income criteria, enrollment process*

- [ ] Are prior authorization support services established?
  - *Hub services, e-PA capabilities, appeal support*

- [ ] Is there adherence support for appropriate products?
  - *Refill reminders, nurse support, patient education*

- [ ] Are patient support metrics tracked and optimized?
  - *Enrollment rates, time to therapy, abandonment rates*

## Domain: Field Force Readiness

**Context:** Preparedness of the sales organization to execute launch
**Value:** Well-prepared field force accelerates adoption and maximizes launch impact
**Risk:** Unprepared field force wastes launch window and undermines HCP confidence

### Practice: Territory Mapping

**Context:** Geographic alignment of sales resources to market opportunity
**Value:** Optimal territory design balances workload, coverage, and opportunity capture
**Risk:** Poor alignment leaves opportunity uncovered or resources misallocated

#### Questions

- [ ] Are territories aligned to target HCP concentration?
  - *Not just geographic; aligned to prescriber opportunity*

- [ ] Is call capacity realistic for territory size and travel requirements?
  - *Appropriate number of targets per rep; drive time considered*

- [ ] Are high-opportunity territories appropriately resourced?
  - *More resources where more opportunity exists*

- [ ] Is institutional/academic coverage addressed appropriately?
  - *Specialty resources for complex accounts*

- [ ] Is territory alignment coordinated with other therapeutic areas?
  - *Cross-portfolio efficiency where relevant*

### Practice: Message Training

**Context:** Field force education on product, disease, and competitive positioning
**Value:** Well-trained reps deliver consistent, compliant, compelling messages
**Risk:** Undertrained reps damage credibility, create compliance risk, and miss opportunities

#### Questions

- [ ] Is product and disease training comprehensive?
  - *Mechanism, clinical data, treatment landscape, patient profiles*

- [ ] Is competitive training included with positioning guidance?
  - *How to handle competitive situations; compliant contrast*

- [ ] Are selling skills and objection handling practiced?
  - *Role play, certification, coaching*

- [ ] Is training certified with competency assessment?
  - *Not just attendance; demonstrated proficiency*

- [ ] Is ongoing training planned post-launch?
  - *New data, competitive updates, refresher training*

### Practice: KPI Framework

**Context:** Metrics and accountability for field force performance
**Value:** Clear KPIs drive appropriate behaviors and enable performance management
**Risk:** Wrong metrics drive wrong behaviors; lack of accountability limits performance

#### Questions

- [ ] Are leading and lagging indicators defined?
  - *Activity metrics (calls) and outcome metrics (Rx) both tracked*

- [ ] Is incentive compensation aligned with strategic objectives?
  - *IC plan rewards desired behaviors and outcomes*

- [ ] Are call quality metrics incorporated?
  - *Not just quantity; message delivery, discussion quality*

- [ ] Is there a performance management cadence established?
  - *Weekly, monthly, quarterly reviews with coaching*

- [ ] Are field force analytics capabilities in place?
  - *Dashboards, CRM, prescription data accessible to reps and managers*
